Frequently Asked Questions

Does PATHWAY AI replace clinical psychological testing?

No. PATHWAY AI provides transparent, rule-based vocational interest and cognitive aptitude alignment tools. We never diagnose clinical conditions or claim IQ validation.

Can I view my past assessments after logging in again?

Yes! Every assessment attempt, chosen roadmap, skill progress, and payment receipt is saved permanently in the database and retrieved automatically upon login.

How does the ₹99 Analytics Report purchase work?

Students can take the assessment and preview results for free. A one-time fee of ₹99 unlocks the complete analytical dashboard, radar chart breakdowns, and customized gap action plan in Razorpay test mode.

Are the career eligibility rules verified?

Yes. All requirements link back to official statutory bodies such as NMC, AICTE, NTA, ICAI, ICSI, DGCA, and UPSC with documented verification dates.
