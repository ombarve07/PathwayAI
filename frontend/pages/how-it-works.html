Methodology & Design

How PATHWAY AI Works

An explainable, rule-based career guidance framework designed for the nuances of the Indian education system.

Stage 1

Education Level Calibration

Guidance needs are fundamentally different between a 9th standard student choosing their board stream, a 12th standard student preparing for JEE or NEET, a polytechnic diploma holder seeking lateral B.Tech entry, and a college graduate preparing for UPSC or software engineering.

PATHWAY AI asks targeted academic details—including board/university, current subjects, and diploma/degree branch—to ensure subsequent questions are strictly age-appropriate.

Stage 2

The Three Decision Frameworks

Option 1: Fixed Career — For students who already know their target (e.g. "I want to become a Doctor or Software Developer"). Conducts deep profession-specific skill and prerequisite analysis.
Option 2: Not Fixed (Discovery) — For students who feel confused about their strengths. Assesses numerical, logical, verbal, spatial, interest, and situational dimensions to uncover top matches.
Option 3: Career Clarity — For students with 2 or 3 careers in mind. Compares the selected choices side-by-side with required skill weights and alternative matches.
Stage 3

Transparent & Explainable Scoring

We believe career algorithms should be completely transparent rather than opaque black boxes. Scores are separated into:

  • Current Demonstrated Skills: Quantitative problem-solving and domain fundamentals.
  • Interest & Work Preferences: Real task engagement based on RIASEC vocational models.
  • Soft Skills & Situational Judgement: Team collaboration and ethics under stress.
  • Overall Career Compatibility: Weighted percentage alignment (0–100%) against verified requirements.
Stage 4

Three-Part Indian Eligibility Breakdown

We explicitly separate career compatibility from legal admission eligibility:

1. Eligible Now

Milestones and entrance eligibility already unlocked by current education.

2. Not Yet Eligible

Mandatory subjects, minimum percentages, or degrees still missing.

3. Future Eligibility

The exact qualifying route (e.g. NIOS, CET, LEET, graduation) required to qualify.
