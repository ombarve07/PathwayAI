Our Mission

About PATHWAY AI

Empowering Indian students with scientific, transparent, and verified career roadmaps.

The Challenge in Indian Education

Every year in India, over 25 million students finish 10th standard and make life-defining decisions on streams, entrance examinations, and college degrees with minimal structured guidance. Most students rely solely on informal advice from family or commercial coaching institutes.

PATHWAY AI was founded to democratize premier, verified career mentorship for students across Tier 1, Tier 2, and rural India.

Our Ethical AI Principles

  • Zero Pseudo-Science: We do not claim to measure IQ or clinical mental health. We assess structured problem-solving, vocational task preferences, and subject competencies.
  • No Deceptive Probability Claims: We will never state "You have a 95% chance of becoming a Doctor." Instead, we explain: "Your assessment indicates strong alignment with the biological and situational reasoning required for medical training."
  • Verifiable Indian Authorities: All eligibility criteria are referenced from official bodies (NTA, NMC, AICTE, UPSC, ICAI, BCI, CoA) with verification timestamps.
  • Child Privacy First: Built with strict adherence to India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and guardian consent workflows.

Platform Architecture

Built as a high-performance, full-stack application leveraging FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, MySQL, and Apple Minimal Premium responsive design patterns.
