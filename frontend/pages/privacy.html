Privacy Policy & DPDP Compliance

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Protection of Minor Students

PATHWAY AI is committed to student data privacy and complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. For users under 18 years of age, guardian consent is requested during registration to ensure responsible processing of educational preference data.

2. Data Collection Minimization

We only collect data strictly necessary to compute educational and career guidance: full name, email, year of study, academic board/stream, and answers to assessment items. We do not track or sell student location or biometric data.

3. No Sale of Student Information

Student assessment responses and reports are strictly private. We never sell student data to external advertising networks or commercial coaching centers.

4. Right to Erase & Export

Students and parents have the right to request a complete export of their profile data or permanently delete their account at any time via Account Settings.
